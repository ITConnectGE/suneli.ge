The company about spices

   Our company was founded on 15 January, 2014 for the following purpose:
To supply the population of Georgia with ecologically fresh spices.
   Except the local, traditional Georgian spices, we import the spices
of high quality (in seeds) from the worldwide famous industrial
countries: Egypt, India, Vietnam, Bulgaria, the Ukraine.
   We grind, package them and offer the product to the consumers
without any additional appendage in the optimum packing considering
the accessibility of the price.
