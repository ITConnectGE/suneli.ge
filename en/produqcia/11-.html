 Coriander
<p align="center"> </p>
<p>straight stem of about 60 cm. It has spherical or long-round product
containing essential oil</p>
<p> and fats.Coriander has been
spread in Georgia since ancient time and there are several </p>
<p> cultural species. Coriander is
widely used in cookery though it is often used in cosmetics </p>
<p> and medicine.</p><br />
