 Saffron (Yellow flower)


in Europe and south-west Asia. In Georgia are only 5 species and one (Crocus Autranil) among them is narrow endemic species growing in Abkhazia. All species of saffron are with beautiful flowers; they are grown in the decorative gardens. Yellow flower is one of the species of saffron which is widely used in cookery. Saffron has the unique healing properties: it can cure more than 100 diseases.
