 Kharcho


It is traditional Georgian spice mixed with different spices. The title originates from the

traditional Georgian dish ("Kharcho"). It is very popular in the Caucaus countries and

used for cooking cold and hot dishes.

To prepare Kharcho there are various recipes (according to the amount of the

component spices); There are respectively different options for color and flavor.
