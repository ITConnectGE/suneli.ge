 Red pepper

The product is the berries with plenty of seeds: red or dark red, round or long. The home

country of the red pepper is tropic America. There are the local species and the species

from Kutaisi. the pepper is used as in cookery as medicine-pepper stimulates gastric juice

and it improve food processing; it is used for the treatment of vascular expansion.

Pepper juice is also used to treat bites of reptiles.
